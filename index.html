Pairing help

The pedal only accepts the phone it is bonded with. If the connection fails:

  1. In Android Bluetooth settings, forget "WahouWah".
  2. Clear the bond on the pedal (same procedure as with the native app).
  3. Come back here, press CONNECT and accept the pairing request.
